# Parchem Fine & Specialty Chemicals — Prospective Distribution Partner

**Status: Prospective — no existing relationship with CitraChem.** This profile documents Parchem as a partnership *candidate*, not a confirmed partner. Do not cite this page as evidence CitraChem and Parchem have any agreement, LOI, or supply relationship; the only confirmed CitraChem partner on record is Waystone Pharmaceuticals (LOI, April 2025, per `CLAUDE.md`).

## Summary
Parchem Fine & Specialty Chemicals (parchem.com) is a US-based global distributor of specialty and fine chemicals, founded in 1999 and headquartered in New Rochelle, NY. It does not manufacture chemicals itself — it sources and resells raw materials and specialty ingredients into the pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, personal care, food/flavor-fragrance, agricultural-chemical, and adhesives/coatings/paints markets. Parchem already operates a dedicated "Cannabinoid (CBD) and Hemp Products" category within its online Specialty Chemicals Catalog, listing hemp-derived CBD isolate, CBD hemp oil (and a decarboxylated variant), cannabidiolic acid (CBDA), cannabigerol (CBG), and full-spectrum CBD powder as stocked/quotable products — evidence they already run a cannabinoid distribution channel and have the regulatory registrations (DEA, TTB, FDA) to handle it.

## Why a fit for CitraChem
- **Existing cannabinoid distribution infrastructure.** Parchem already lists and quotes CBD isolate, CBD hemp oil, CBDA, and CBG as catalog products, meaning the channel, sales reps, and customer base for cannabinoid ingredients already exist — CitraChem would not be asking Parchem to build a new product category from zero, only to add a higher-purity, non-agricultural source line.
- **Reaches the exact underserved buyer CitraChem's direct sales motion misses.** Parchem's customer base skews toward smaller labs, formulators, and mid-size pharma/nutraceutical buyers who purchase raw materials through a distributor relationship (credit terms, smaller MOQs, one-stop ordering) rather than negotiating direct supply agreements — a complementary channel to CitraChem's direct technical/relationship sales into larger pharma and CRO accounts (per `CLAUDE.md`'s ICP and buying-motion notes).
- **Regulatory posture matches CitraChem's compliance-forward positioning.** Parchem is ISO 9001:2015, USDA Organic, and Kosher certified, and is TTB-, DEA-, ATF-, and FDA-registered, including as a supplier of DEA List I chemicals — the kind of controlled-substance-adjacent handling capability CitraChem's own biomimetic, non-agricultural cannabinoids would need in a distribution partner.
- **Pharma/excipient distribution experience.** Parchem separately supplies pharmaceutical ingredients and excipients into drug formulation/development/production, and is listed as an API-adjacent supplier on pharma-sourcing directories (PharmaCompass, Pharmaceutical Technology's marketplace) — relevant credibility for carrying an API-grade cannabinoid line to pharma-adjacent buyers.
- **Differentiation angle for the pitch, if pursued.** Parchem's current CBD/CBG listings read as hemp/agricultural-extract sourced (standard CAS-referenced isolates and oils), which is exactly the batch-variability, agricultural-impurity category CitraChem's biomimetic process is positioned against in `CLAUDE.md`. A pitch to Parchem would frame CitraChem's PhytoCules line as a higher-purity, non-agricultural upgrade slot alongside (or replacing) their existing cannabinoid SKUs, not a new, unproven category for their sales team.
