# ProVerde Laboratories, Inc. — Prospective Analytical/Testing Partner

**Status: Prospective — no existing relationship with CitraChem.** This profile documents ProVerde as a partnership *candidate*, not a confirmed partner. Do not cite this page as evidence CitraChem and ProVerde have any agreement, contract, or testing relationship; the only confirmed CitraChem partner on record is Waystone Pharmaceuticals (LOI, April 2025, per `CLAUDE.md`).

## Summary
ProVerde Laboratories, Inc. is an ISO/IEC 17025:2017-accredited analytical testing laboratory headquartered at 420 Fortune Blvd, Milford, Massachusetts, founded in 2013 by Dr. Christopher Hudalla (Founder & Chief Scientific Officer). Hudalla is an analytical chemist with 25+ years of experience in chromatographic method development, including 14 years at Waters Corporation (also headquartered in Milford, MA), a PhD from UC Santa Barbara, and a postdoctoral fellowship at the Eppley Institute for Cancer Research (University of Nebraska Medical Center). ProVerde uses HPLC/UPLC/GC/SFC for cannabinoid and terpene potency profiling, LC/MS/MS and GC/MS/MS for pesticide screening, ICP-MS for heavy-metal/elemental analysis, and additional methods for residual solvents, mycotoxins, and microbiological contamination. It is licensed by the Massachusetts Cannabis Control Commission as an Independent Testing Laboratory for medical and adult-use cannabis, but its stated industries served extend beyond cannabis/hemp compliance testing to pharmaceutical, biotechnology, diagnostics, pharmacogenetics, food & nutrition, environmental, and clinical research sectors — i.e., it positions itself as a general separation-sciences/analytical-chemistry lab that happens to also hold cannabis-specific state licensure, not a retail-compliance-only shop. The company is small (roughly 12-13 employees as of mid-2026 per third-party data aggregators), consistent with a boutique specialty lab rather than a large multinational CRO.

## Why a fit for CitraChem
- **Same-state (Massachusetts) footprint.** ProVerde is headquartered in Milford, MA; CitraChem is headquartered in Concord, MA (per `CLAUDE.md`). A local, in-state independent lab is a genuine logistical and relationship-building advantage for routine third-party COA turnaround, on-site audits, and sample chain-of-custody — worth flagging as a "local partner" angle, though no existing relationship should be implied.
- **ISO/IEC 17025:2017 accreditation.** This is the specific accreditation standard CitraChem's positioning (`CLAUDE.md`) points to for credible third-party COA validation of purity/potency and freedom from agricultural impurities — ProVerde holds it (Perry Johnson Laboratory Accreditation, certificate #80585), giving any COA it issues external, auditable standing rather than an in-house-only claim.
- **Right analytical technique set for the purity claim CitraChem needs validated.** ProVerde's chromatographic (HPLC/UPLC/GC/SFC) potency and terpene profiling, LC/MS/MS and GC/MS/MS pesticide screening, ICP-MS heavy-metal analysis, and residual-solvent/mycotoxin/microbiological testing map directly onto the >98%-purity and "free of agricultural impurities (pesticides, heavy metals)" claims in CitraChem's value proposition — a third-party COA from this panel would substantiate, not just restate, that claim.
- **Credibility beyond retail cannabis compliance.** ProVerde's founder's background (14 years at Waters Corporation building the chromatography instruments the industry relies on, PhD-level academic credentials, cancer-research postdoc) and the lab's self-described service to pharmaceutical, biotech, diagnostics, and clinical-research clients — not only state-mandated dispensary/flower compliance testing — support using it for pharma-grade API-adjacent validation work, not just consumer-product batch release. This is the fit question the task flagged as a risk, and the evidence found supports ProVerde as more than a retail-compliance-only lab, though CitraChem should still confirm ProVerde's specific experience with GMP-grade API characterization (vs. cannabis-product compliance panels) before relying on it for a regulatory submission.
- **Scale caveat.** At roughly a dozen employees, ProVerde is a boutique lab — well suited as an independent second-opinion/spot-check COA partner alongside CitraChem's own QC, but likely not a substitute for a large-scale CRO analytical partner if CitraChem needs high-throughput GMP batch release at commercial volume; see the Eurofins cannabis/hemp testing division as a larger-scale alternative if that need arises.
