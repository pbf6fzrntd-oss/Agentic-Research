## Summary
The Association of Cannabinoid Specialists (cannaspecialists.org) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, international association of healthcare professionals working in cannabinoid medicine, founded in 2018 by cannabis physicians ("established 2018" per the organization's own site). It is led by founder and president Dr. Jordan Tishler, MD — an internist, former VA emergency physician, and faculty at Harvard Medical School/Mass General Brigham, who also runs the Massachusetts cannabis-medicine clinic InhaleMD. ACS's stated mission is to set and safeguard clinical standards in cannabis medicine, educate clinicians, lawmakers, and patients, and advocate to regulators that cannabis should be treated and regulated as a medicine rather than folded into recreational-use frameworks. Its membership spans cannabis physicians, nurses, pharmacists, lawyers, and other industry professionals and students, and it runs member education (a Thinkific-hosted course platform, cannaspecialists.org/courses), policy programming (e.g., an "ACS Presents: A Policy Panel on Rescheduling and Medical Cannabis" event), and outward-facing content (YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, X). ACS is still active as of this research: it appears as a listed sponsor/participant in the annual LabRoots "Cannabis Sciences" virtual event series, including the 9th annual edition scheduled for April 15, 2026.
