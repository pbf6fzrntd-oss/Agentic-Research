# Cambrex Corporation — Prospective CDMO Partner

**Status: Prospective — no existing relationship with CitraChem.** This profile documents Cambrex as a partnership *candidate*, not a confirmed partner. Do not cite this page as evidence CitraChem and Cambrex have any agreement, supply arrangement, or manufacturing services contract; the only confirmed CitraChem partner on record is Waystone Pharmaceuticals (LOI, April 2025, per `CLAUDE.md`).

## Summary
Cambrex Corporation is a small-molecule-focused contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO), founded in 1981 and headquartered in East Rutherford, NJ, providing drug substance (API), drug product, and analytical services across the full small-molecule development lifecycle. It has been privately held by Permira since the completion of a take-private acquisition in December 2019. Cambrex is not a cannabinoid manufacturer or distributor — its public capability pages and press activity describe complex API process chemistry (opioids, controlled stimulants, benzodiazepines, general small-molecule APIs) with no evidence of cannabinoid, terpene, or hemp-derived product manufacturing — so it does not compete with CitraChem's PhytoCules or custom-synthesis lines. Its High Point, NC facility, expanded into a dedicated "Center of Excellence" for API clinical supply and process development, is DEA-licensed to handle Schedule II–IV (with Schedule II–V cited on Cambrex's own controlled-substances page) controlled substances and runs multi-step synthetic API production from milligram to 100 kg batch scale for Phase I–III clinical supply, alongside kilo-lab and pilot-scale vessels and continuous-flow reaction capability.

## Why a fit for CitraChem
- **Complex multi-step API synthesis at scale, without cannabinoid conflict.** Cambrex's core capability — multi-step synthetic process chemistry for complex small-molecule APIs, scaling from mg to 100 kg batches — is a direct structural match for scaling up CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis process beyond its own facility, without Cambrex itself competing in cannabinoid or terpene manufacturing.
- **Existing DEA controlled-substance manufacturing licenses.** Cambrex's High Point, NC site already holds DEA licenses for process research, development, manufacturing, and importation of scheduled controlled substances (Schedule II–IV/V) and has manufactured controlled compounds (including potent opioids) since 2005 — the regulatory and containment infrastructure a phytocannabinoid API scale-up partner would need, already in place rather than requiring CitraChem to qualify a new licensee from scratch.
- **Clinical-to-commercial scale-up track record.** The High Point site was purpose-expanded as a "Center of Excellence" for API clinical supply and process development, with kilo-scale and pilot-scale vessels and continuous-flow production — matching the trajectory of a growing pharma/biotech-facing supplier like CitraChem moving research-scale custom-synthesis orders toward commercial-volume supply.
- **Life-sciences-only focus with no cannabinoid product line.** Cambrex's public materials position it purely as a small-molecule therapeutics CDMO (drug substance, drug product, analytical services) serving pharma/biotech sponsors — no cannabinoid, terpene, or hemp-derived catalog was found, keeping it outside the competitor set (Purisys/Noramco, GVB Biopharma, Avicanna, Willow Biosciences, InMed/BayMedica, CB Therapeutics, Lygos) this batch is tracking separately.

## Facts
- Cambrex Corporation is a small-molecule CDMO founded in 1981, headquartered in East Rutherford, NJ, and privately owned by Permira since its take-private acquisition closed December 4, 2019 (~1,900–2,000 employees).
- Cambrex's High Point, NC facility is DEA-licensed for process research, development, manufacturing, and importation of controlled substances (Schedule II–IV/V per Cambrex's own site) and has manufactured potent opioids since 2005.
- Cambrex produces complex, multi-step-synthesis APIs and intermediates at the High Point site in batch sizes from milligrams to 100 kg, supporting Phase I–III clinical supply, with kilo-lab, pilot-scale, and continuous-flow reaction capability.
- No public evidence of cannabinoid, terpene, or hemp-derived product manufacturing by Cambrex was found; its published capabilities are small-molecule pharmaceutical APIs and drug product/analytical services.
- No public evidence found of any existing relationship between Cambrex and CitraChem; this profile is prospective only.
