# Waystone Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

## Summary
Waystone Pharmaceuticals, Inc. is a pre-clinical pharmaceutical company based in San Diego, CA (incorporated January 31, 2024), developing a library of small-molecule therapeutics targeting the endocannabinoid system (ECS). Its lead program targets inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) and was described as ready for Phase I studies as of the April 2025 announcement; a second pre-clinical program targets metabolic disorders (type 2 diabetes) using a novel CB1 neutral antagonist alone and in combination with a GLP-1 agonist. On April 8, 2025, CitraChem, Corp. and Waystone announced a signed Letter of Intent (LOI) to jointly build a library of new natural-product derivatives targeting IBD, with the goal of discovering a novel drug candidate — CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis platform supplying the cannabinoid variants Waystone needs faster than the alternative routes it was evaluating.

## Why a fit for CitraChem
- Waystone is CitraChem's precedent deal shape for the co-development/pharma-partner ICP described in `CLAUDE.md`: a pre-clinical company building ECS-targeted small-molecule therapeutics that needs a dependable, high-purity source of cannabinoid variants rather than agricultural extraction or slow de novo total synthesis.
- CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis platform lets Waystone access a broader library of natural-product/cannabinoid derivatives (including rare/non-major cannabinoids) faster than the alternative synthesis routes it was considering, per CSO Jason Kingsbury's comment in the announcement.
- A validated case study for CitraChem's custom-synthesis service and its pitch to other pre-clinical ECS-focused biotech and pharma BD leads evaluating a supply relationship.
