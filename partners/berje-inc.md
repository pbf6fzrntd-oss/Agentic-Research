# Berjé Inc.

**Status: Prospective partner — no existing relationship with CitraChem has been found or is implied by this profile.**

## Summary
Berjé Inc. is a family-owned essential oils and aromatic chemicals supplier headquartered in Carteret, New Jersey, founded in 1949 and now run by its third generation. It supplies over 300 essential oils and more than 2,500 natural and synthetic aromatic chemicals — including named monoterpene fractions (e.g., orange terpenes, lime terpenes, eucalyptus oil terpenes, spearmint terpenes, vetiver terpenes) — sourced from over 80 countries, and holds SQF (Safe Quality Food) Food Safety Code Edition 9.0 certification plus over 1,000 Kosher- and Halal-certified items. As a plant-based essential-oil and terpene supplier at industrial scale, Berjé is a candidate upstream raw-material source for the botanical precursors CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis process starts from — not a customer or a competing cannabinoid manufacturer.

## Why a fit for CitraChem
- CLAUDE.md states CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis process starts "from plant-based essential oil precursors, not cannabis cultivation or agricultural extraction" — that makes a large, quality-documented essential-oil/terpene supplier a genuine upstream input vendor, structurally distinct from CitraChem's pharma/nutraceutical customers and from its semi-synthesis/biosynthesis competitors (e.g. Purisys, Noramco, Willow Biosciences-style platforms).
- Berjé's catalog includes named monoterpene/terpene fractions (citrus terpenes, eucalyptus terpenes, spearmint terpenes, vetiver terpenes, etc.) at industrial volume — the class of botanical starting materials a terpene- and terpenophenolic-derivative-focused semi-synthesis process would plausibly draw on, though CitraChem's confirmed process specifics and which named precursor(s) it actually sources are not public and are not claimed here.
- Berjé's SQF Food Safety Code Edition 9.0 certification, HACCP/GMP-based testing, and stated per-container QC/traceability standards are the kind of documented quality system a pharma-adjacent, regulated buyer (CitraChem) would need from an upstream supplier to keep its own COA and purity claims (>98% purity, free of agricultural impurities) defensible back to the raw material.
- Scale (5.5M+ kg of inventory on hand, sourcing from 80+ countries) suggests Berjé could support CitraChem's stated ambition to supply "research and commercial scale" volumes without precursor-supply becoming CitraChem's own bottleneck — this is an inference from Berjé's stated scale, not a verified capacity commitment.
- Caveat: no public source confirms Berjé sells directly to pharmaceutical API manufacturers, confirms pharma-grade (vs. food/flavor-grade) specifications for any specific terpene, or confirms the exact precursor(s) CitraChem's biomimetic route requires — a real due-diligence pass would need to verify pharma-grade spec availability and any regulatory (e.g., DEA precursor-control) considerations directly with Berjé before this moves beyond prospective.
