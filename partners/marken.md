# Marken (a UPS Healthcare company)

**Status: Prospective partner candidate — no relationship with CitraChem currently exists or is implied.**

## Summary
Marken is a global clinical trial logistics and specimen-management company, acquired by UPS in November 2016 and now operated as part of UPS Healthcare. It specializes in GMP-compliant storage, distribution, and courier handling of clinical trial materials — including cold chain, deep-frozen, and controlled-ambient shipping — through a worldwide depot network, and holds site-level licensing in specific markets to handle DEA-scheduled and other controlled substances as part of clinical-trial drug supply. It is one of the best-documented names in the "clinical-trial-grade, controlled-substance-capable logistics" category alongside World Courier (AmerisourceBergen/Cencora).

## Why a fit for CitraChem
- CitraChem's phytocannabinoid and terpenophenolic products (PhytoCules catalog and custom-synthesis deliverables) ship to pharma/biotech R&D, CROs, and academic labs — the same investigator-site and central-lab network Marken already serves for clinical trial materials.
- Some cannabinoid APIs and precursor/intermediate compounds fall under controlled-substance scheduling depending on jurisdiction and final molecule; Marken's site-level authorization to handle DEA-scheduled (Schedule IV-V) and cytotoxic/highly potent substances (documented at its Moscow depot) and its Korea depot's Wholesale Dealers License show it already operates the compliance infrastructure (chain-of-custody, licensed depots, import/export handling) this shipping profile requires, rather than CitraChem building that capability itself.
- Cold-chain and temperature-controlled handling (controlled ambient through deep-frozen/LN2) matches the stability needs of purified cannabinoid/terpene APIs during clinical or research-scale shipment.
- A logistics partner already trusted by CitraChem's own ICP (pharma/biotech sponsors, CROs, academic labs) reduces friction and de-risks a buyer's due diligence on supply-chain integrity for a regulated ingredient.

## Facts
- Marken is a clinical trial logistics and drug-depot company; UPS acquired Marken in November 2016; it now operates as part of UPS Healthcare.
- Marken's depot network is described as 12 fully-owned and 12 partner locations, expanded to 58 total locations worldwide (per 2021 UPS Healthcare cold chain network expansion announcement).
- Marken's Moscow depot is authorized for wholesale trade of drug substances including Schedule IV-V controlled substances and cytotoxic/highly potent substances within clinical trials; Marken Korea's Clinical Supply Center operates under a Wholesale Dealers License (WDL).
- Marken states it manages clinical trial material logistics for roughly 45,000 investigator sites, hundreds of drug-development companies, and around 40 central labs globally.
- Marken offers controlled-ambient, cool chain, cold chain, deep-frozen, and LN2 temperature-controlled storage and distribution.
- No public record found of any partnership, agreement, or relationship between Marken and CitraChem as of 2026-09-16.
