## Summary
Delphian Therapeutics (Chicago, IL; formerly Schedule 1 Therapeutics, founded 2018) is a venture-backed, clinical-stage biotech developing **S1-220**, a 100:1 CBD:THC fixed-dose combination formulated as a sublingual spray for migraine, built on an SBIR/STTR-funded Phase I/II partnership with the University of Iowa (Andrew Russo lab). The company is targeting FDA IND clearance and a Phase 1 trial against triptan-failure migraine patients. Founder/CEO **George Pappas** is also the named inventor on two issued US patents assigned to the company — US 11,712,431 B2 and US 12,029,721 B2, both titled "Compositions comprising cannabinoids and methods of use" — which is independent, primary-source corroboration of his role beyond the LinkedIn/search-snippet evidence already used for the first touch. This follow-up pass could not find (a) any public contact channel for Delphian beyond LinkedIn and a corporate mailing address, (b) a clearly-identified lead investor or named board member more reachable than Pappas himself, or (c) any public information on Delphian's current cannabinoid API sourcing or manufacturing approach for S1-220 — each is detailed below with what was and wasn't verifiable.

- Delphian's S1-220 is built on the exact fixed-ratio (100:1 CBD:THC) science CitraChem's platform is positioned to serve consistently and without agricultural batch variability — the same differentiator CLAUDE.md frames against plant extraction.
