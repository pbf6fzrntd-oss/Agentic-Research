# Battlecard: Cerilliant Corporation

**Last checked:** 2026-09-16
**Category:** Analytical reference standards / certified reference materials (CRMs) — a different competitive tier than CitraChem's research/commercial-scale API and ingredient manufacturing. Included because CitraChem's ICP includes academic/government research labs, and a researcher sourcing "pure CBG" or "pure THC" may compare a milligram-scale certified reference standard against a research/commercial-scale ingredient supplier without realizing they're different product categories.

## Summary
Cerilliant Corporation (Round Rock, TX) manufactures certified reference standards and certified reference materials (CRMs) — including a cannabinoid product line (CBD, CBN, THCV, Δ9-THC, Δ8-THC, THCA and related acids/metabolites, plus stable-isotope-labeled internal standards like CBD-D3, THCA-D3, Δ9-THC-D3) sold as precisely-quantified, dilute solutions (typically ~1 mg/mL, in 0.5–1 mL ampules) for GC/MS and LC/MS instrument calibration, method validation, and potency/QC testing — not for formulation, dosing, or bulk manufacturing input. Cerilliant was founded in 1980 (formerly the Analytical Reference Materials Division of Radian International), acquired by Sigma-Aldrich Corporation in December 2010, and has been part of Merck KGaA's life-science business (operating as MilliporeSigma/Supelco) since Merck acquired Sigma-Aldrich in 2015. Cerilliant is a registered trademark of Sigma-Aldrich Co. LLC; its cannabinoid CRMs are sold through the Sigma-Aldrich/MilliporeSigma/Supelco e-commerce catalog and distributors (Fisher Scientific, VWR, Thomas Scientific). [Source: Wikipedia — Cerilliant Corporation, accessed 2026-09-16; Cerilliant company profile via search snippet, accessed 2026-09-16]

**This is an analytical-reference-standard supplier, not a bulk/API ingredient manufacturer.** Its cannabinoid products are calibration/QC tools sold in milligram quantities per ampule, priced and packaged for laboratory instrument use — a fundamentally different scale and use case than CitraChem's research-to-commercial-scale phytocannabinoid, terpene, and custom terpenophenolic derivative supply.

## Capabilities
- Cannabinoid CRM catalog spans neutral and acidic phytocannabinoids (CBD, CBN, CBG, CBDV, CBL, CBC, THCV, Δ8-THC, Δ9-THC, THCA, CBDA, CBGA, 7-COOH-CBD and other metabolites), stable-isotope-labeled internal standards (e.g., CBD-D3, CBN-D3, THCA-D3, Δ9-THC-D3), and pre-mixed multi-analyte calibration panels (3-, 8-, 17-component mixtures). [Source: Sigma-Aldrich "Cannabis Standards" page and Cerilliant product pages, via search, accessed 2026-09-16]
- Accreditations: ISO Guide 34 and ISO/IEC 17025 accredited; ISO 13485 and ISO 9001 certified; also described as meeting ISO 17034:2016 (Reference Material Producer) requirements. Marketed with "double accreditation to ISO/IEC 17025 and ISO 17034." [Source: Cerilliant company profile / accreditation certificate PDF hosted on sigmaaldrich.com, via search, accessed 2026-09-16]
- Handles Schedule I controlled-substance material (cannabinoids/THC fall under CSA drug codes 7360 "Marihuana" and 7370 "Tetrahydrocannabinols") under DEA registration. Federal Register shows Cerilliant has an active/pending 2026 DEA application to register as a **Bulk Manufacturer of Controlled Substances** for drug codes 7360 and 7370 (as synthetic substances), published July 22, 2026 (Federal Register Vol. 91, No. 139, p. 46169; comment/hearing-request window through September 21, 2026), and a separate pending **Importer of Controlled Substances** application published June 15, 2026 (Federal Register Vol. 91, No. 114, pp. 36003–36005). An earlier Bulk Manufacturer application from Cerilliant was also published July 17, 2025. These are **applications, not yet confirmed final DEA registrations**, as of this check. [Source: federalregister.gov notices, via search snippets, accessed 2026-09-16 — confirmed via primary federal filing, though full document text could not be directly fetched in this pass due to network access restrictions]
- Serves forensic/toxicology, clinical/diagnostic, environmental, natural-products/nutraceutical, and pharmaceutical analytical markets broadly (not cannabinoid-exclusive) — cannabinoids are one product line within a much larger reference-standard catalog (drugs of abuse, pharmaceuticals, environmental contaminants, etc.). [Source: Cerilliant company profile, via search, accessed 2026-09-16]

## Where CitraChem wins
- **Scale and purpose mismatch works in CitraChem's favor for any buyer who actually needs material, not a calibration standard.** Cerilliant's cannabinoid products are milligram-scale, ampule-packaged analytical solutions for instrument calibration/QC — they are not a source of supply for formulation development, dosing studies, tox/pharm work requiring meaningful mass, or any commercial/bulk use. CitraChem supplies research-scale samples through commercial/bulk quantities of >98%-pure phytocannabinoids and terpenes for exactly those use cases.
- **Custom/rare molecules and derivatives.** Cerilliant's catalog is built around known, named cannabinoids and their common metabolites/isotope-labeled analogs for existing test methods — it is not a custom synthesis service. CitraChem's custom synthesis line produces novel/rare cannabinoids and bespoke terpenophenolic derivatives to a client's own specification (via citrachem.com/custom/), which Cerilliant's reference-standard business model does not address.
- **Production philosophy.** CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis from plant-based essential-oil precursors is positioned against agricultural extraction and de novo total synthesis; Cerilliant's role is calibration/verification standards, not a manufacturing route claim, so this axis doesn't apply — but it's a distinction worth making explicit to a researcher comparing the two: Cerilliant doesn't compete on "how is this cannabinoid made," only on "here is a certified small amount of it for instrument calibration."

## Where they win / honest gaps
- **Certified-reference-material accreditation is Cerilliant's core competency and a real, narrow moat.** ISO Guide 34, ISO/IEC 17025, and ISO 17034 accreditation for reference material production is a specific regulatory/quality credential that analytical labs performing potency testing, forensic toxicology, or method validation are often required to source from — CLAUDE.md does not claim this accreditation for CitraChem, and this battlecard should not imply CitraChem competes on certified-reference-material accreditation. **This is a genuine gap, not a claim to paper over.**
- **DEA-licensed handling of Schedule I cannabinoid/THC material is an operating credential Cerilliant has actively pursued (pending 2026 bulk-manufacturer and importer applications) that a buyer needing DEA-compliant sourcing documentation may specifically require** — verify current registration status directly with Cerilliant/DEA before citing this as settled, since the 2026 filings found in this research were pending applications, not confirmed grants, as of 2026-09-16.
- **Backed by Merck KGaA/MilliporeSigma's distribution network** (Fisher Scientific, VWR, Thomas Scientific, direct Sigma-Aldrich e-commerce) — a researcher can order a cannabinoid reference standard through familiar existing lab-supply accounts with standard shipping/logistics, which is a real convenience CitraChem, as a direct quote-based seller, does not offer in the same self-serve form (CitraChem's motion is request-a-sample/custom-inquiry, not catalog checkout — see CLAUDE.md and playbooks/RFQ-PLAYBOOK.md).
- **This is not an apples-to-apples competitor for most CitraChem deals.** For any prospect whose actual need is research/commercial-scale material (formulation, dosing, pilot/scale-up, drug development), Cerilliant is not a substitute and should not be framed as one — the honest comparison only applies to a buyer explicitly asking about instrument-calibration or CRM-grade reference material.
