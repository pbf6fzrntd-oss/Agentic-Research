## Summary

Cayman Chemical is an Ann Arbor, Michigan life-sciences reagent company, founded 1980, that sells biochemicals, assay kits, antibodies, and recombinant proteins to academic and pharma research labs, alongside contract chemical-synthesis and drug-discovery services. Within cannabinoids, it runs two catalog lines relevant to CitraChem's ICP: "Cannabinoid Research Tools" (probes/reagents for endocannabinoid-system research, going back to the 1980s) and "Cannabis & Hemp Analytical Standards" (ISO 17034/ISO 17025-accredited certified reference materials and neat single-compound standards for potency testing, terpene profiling, and contaminant analysis, sold since it began producing synthetic-cannabinoid forensic standards around 2009). These are analytical-reference-grade products — sold in milligram-to-tens-of-milligrams neat quantities or as µg/mL solutions in sealed ampules — not bulk industrial or clinical-scale API supply. Separately, Cayman Chemical (parent company) also runs a cGMP API/CDMO business, but that arm's demonstrated commercial-scale manufacturing expertise is in prostaglandin APIs via its Czech subsidiary Cayman Pharma s.r.o., not cannabinoids. As of a Federal Register notice published November 26, 2025, Cayman Chemical has an application pending with the DEA (filed August 20, 2025) to become a registered bulk manufacturer of controlled substances under drug codes 7360 (Marihuana) and 7370 (Tetrahydrocannabinols), stated purpose including forensic reference standards and "Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients for Phase 1 trials" — a signal of intent to move into small-scale cannabinoid API supply, but as of this check it is an unapproved application (comment/objection period ran through January 26, 2026), not a live capability.

## Features

- "Cannabinoid Research Tools" catalog: reagents, assay kits, antibodies, and screening tools for endocannabinoid-system (CB1/CB2 receptor, FAAH, MAGL, etc.) research, positioned for basic-science and pharmacology labs. Source: caymanchem.com/cannabinoidresearchtools (via search index), checked 2026-09-16.
- "Cannabis & Hemp Analytical Standards" catalog: single-component phytocannabinoid and terpene reference standards (e.g., Δ9-THC, Δ8-THC, CBD, CBG, CBDV, cannabinoid acids), deuterated internal standards, and pre-made multi-component CRM mixtures for potency testing, terpene profiling, mycotoxin/residual-solvent/pesticide contaminant analysis. Source: caymanchem.com/cannabisandhempanalyticalstandards (via search index), checked 2026-09-16.
- Reference materials are produced under ISO/IEC 17025:2017 and ISO 17034:2016 accreditation (ANAB-accredited), meaning certified concentrations carry documented measurement uncertainty and metrological traceability — a genuine strength for analytical/forensic use cases. Source: ANAB accreditation as described on reseller/distributor pages (bioscience.co.uk, Cambridge Bioscience) referencing Cayman's certification, checked 2026-09-16.
- Separate "Chemical Synthesis" and "APIs & CDMO Services" business lines offer custom synthesis and cGMP contract manufacturing generally (caymanchem.com/services/chemicalsynthesis, caymanchem.com/apis/overview) — but no evidence found in this session that cannabinoids specifically are offered through the cGMP/commercial-scale CDMO arm; Cayman's demonstrated cGMP scale-up track record (via Cayman Pharma s.r.o.) is in prostaglandins for ophthalmic, veterinary, vascular, and psychedelic-therapeutic applications, not cannabinoids. Checked 2026-09-16.
- Broad general reagent company: also sells lipids, assay kits, antibodies, recombinant proteins, and provides contract drug-discovery/screening services outside cannabinoids — cannabinoids are one product category within a much larger life-sciences catalog. Source: company overview via search index (Wikipedia/company profile aggregators), checked 2026-09-16.

## Where CitraChem wins

- **Scale:** CitraChem supplies research-scale through commercial/bulk quantities of phytocannabinoids and terpenophenolic derivatives from one biomimetic semi-synthesis platform. Cayman's cannabinoid catalog, by contrast, is built for analytical-reference use — milligram neat-solid pack sizes and µg/mL solution ampules for a lab's assay or QC bench, not gram-to-kilogram formulation or drug-development feedstock. A buyer who needs more than reference material runs into a scale ceiling Cayman's cannabinoid catalog wasn't built to clear (its cGMP-scale manufacturing expertise is demonstrated in prostaglandins, not cannabinoids).
- **Custom/rare cannabinoids and derivatives:** CitraChem's custom synthesis service produces novel or rare cannabinoids and bespoke terpenophenolic derivatives to a client's own specification. Cayman's cannabinoid business is a standards/reagent catalog of known, previously characterized compounds (plus a general-purpose chemical-synthesis service not specific to cannabinoid derivatives) — it is not positioned as a source for a client's own novel candidate molecule at any scale.
- **Purity/process consistency without agricultural inputs at production scale:** CitraChem's biomimetic route avoids cannabis cultivation and agricultural-extract variability at the volumes a formulation or drug-development program actually consumes; Cayman's analytical standards are, by design, small enough quantities that this comparison doesn't really apply to them.

## Where they win / honest gaps

- **Accreditation depth for QC/forensic use:** Cayman's ISO/IEC 17025 + ISO 17034 dual accreditation on its CRMs is a specific, credible strength for a lab's potency-testing or forensic-chain-of-custody workflow that CitraChem should not claim to match without its own equivalent accreditation on file — verify CitraChem's own CRM/CofA accreditation status before claiming parity here.
- **Breadth of reference catalog and turnkey mixtures:** Cayman offers a wide range of pre-made multi-component CRM mixtures (e.g., 10+ phytocannabinoids in one ampule) tuned for specific HPLC/GC-MS/LC-MS workflows — a convenience for a QC or analytical-method lab that isn't CitraChem's focus (CitraChem is upstream: producing the ingredient, not the analytical-reference kit).
- **Established brand and channel:** Founded 1980, broad reseller network (LGC Standards, Biomol, Sanbio, Cambridge Bioscience, and others carry its catalog), and decades of standing in the cannabinoid-receptor research community going back to the 1980s — an established-trust advantage with academic labs that a newer supplier has to earn.
- **Possible upmarket move (watch item, unconfirmed):** the pending DEA bulk-manufacturer application referencing THC API for Phase 1 trials (filed Aug 2025, published Nov 2025) suggests Cayman may be positioning to offer small-scale cannabinoid API supply beyond pure reference standards. This is not yet a granted registration or a live product as of this check — re-verify at the next refresh (check DEA/Federal Register for a grant or denial, and caymanchem.com/apis for any cannabinoid-specific CDMO listing).
