# Battlecard: Avicanna Inc. (incl. Santa Marta Golden Hemp / Aureus)

**Category:** Agricultural (plant) extraction — vertically integrated grower/extractor + finished-product pharma/consumer company (a different shape than a pure ingredient supplier)
**Last checked:** 2026-09-16

## Summary

Avicanna Inc. is a Toronto-headquartered, commercial-stage international biopharmaceutical company that develops, manufactures, and sells cannabinoid-based medical, pharmaceutical, and consumer products (TSX: AVCN; OTCQX: AVCNF; Frankfurt: 0NN). Unlike CitraChem, Avicanna is vertically integrated top to bottom: it owns a majority stake in Colombian cultivation/extraction subsidiaries — Santa Marta Golden Hemp S.A.S. (SMGH) and Sativa Nativa S.A.S. — that grow and extract cannabis/hemp into CBD, THC, and CBG (sold under the "Aureus" API brand), and it also formulates and sells its own finished products (RHO Phyto medical line, Pura Earth/Pura Hemp topicals, the Trunerox epilepsy drug, and Canadian medical-cannabis distribution via MyMedi.ca). Avicanna's own disclosed FY2025 revenue split (~CAD 23.6M Canada vs. ~CAD 1.9M International) suggests the SMGH extraction/API-export business is a minority contributor to overall company revenue relative to Avicanna's Canadian medical-cannabis/consumer-product operations — inferred from the geographic revenue breakdown, not stated by Avicanna as a segment label. [Source: StratCann summary of Avicanna FY2025 audited results, referencing GlobeNewswire release, checked 2026-09-16]

## Pricing / Sourcing model

- No public price list was found for Aureus API (CBD/THC/CBG isolates/extracts) or for finished RHO Phyto/Pura Earth products; commercial API supply appears to be a B2B relationship (export contracts to international partners), consistent with typical cannabinoid-ingredient sourcing, but no per-kg or per-batch pricing is published. **Inferred, not confirmed** — flag to other agents: do not assume a price point for Avicanna/Aureus API.
- Finished consumer/medical products (RHO Phyto, Pura Earth, MyMedi.ca catalog) are sold through Canadian medical-cannabis and retail channels and are reimbursement-linked in part to Veterans Affairs Canada (VAC) rates — Avicanna's own Q2 2026 release attributes a revenue decline partly to an April 1, 2026 VAC reimbursement rate cut. [Source: Avicanna Q2 2026 financial results press release, Nasdaq/GlobeNewswire, Aug 14, 2026, checked 2026-09-16]
- Avicanna is publicly traded and has raised capital via non-brokered private placements in June 2026 and August 2026. [Source: GlobeNewswire press releases, June 12, 2026 and Aug 19, 2026, checked 2026-09-16]

## Capabilities / Features

- **Cultivation + extraction (SMGH, Santa Marta, Colombia):** majority-owned (60%) subsidiary per a binding agreement executed March 5, 2018; grows and extracts hemp/cannabis into CBD, THC, and CBG. [Source: PR Newswire/Newswire.ca, "Avicanna Provides Details About the Santa Marta Golden Hemp Transaction," 2018, checked 2026-09-16]
- **Aureus API/ingredient brand:** CBD, THC, and CBG extracts/isolates offered at two spec tiers — "pharmaceutical" (claimed EU/US pharmacopeia-compliant) and "cosmetic." Marketed exports to 19 countries, including commercial exports to the US, Germany, Portugal, and Spain. [Source: avicanna.com/Aureus Santa Marta site content via search snippet; GlobeNewswire export press releases 2022-2023, checked 2026-09-16 — primary-source company claim, not independently verified against a COA by this battlecard]
- **Pharmaceutical pipeline:** Trunerox, a proprietary 10% oral CBD formulation manufactured GMP at Altea Farmacéutica (Bogotá) using SMGH-sourced CBD, received Colombia's first indication-specific drug registration from INVIMA in Q1 2024, for adjuvant treatment of seizures associated with Lennox-Gastaut and Dravet syndromes; commercialization in Colombia began in late 2025. [Source: GlobeNewswire, "Avicanna Obtains Its First Indication-Specific Drug Registration with Trunerox," Feb 13, 2024, checked 2026-09-16]
- **Consumer/medical brands:** RHO Phyto (Canadian medical cannabis formulary — oils, sprays, capsules, creams, gels, GMP-manufactured), Pura Earth/Pura Hemp topicals, and the QUIX Rapid-Onset Medical Cannabis Portfolio launched July 16, 2026. Distribution includes Avicanna's own MyMedi.ca platform and Shoppers Drug Mart's medical cannabis channel. [Source: avicanna.com press releases via search; Q1/Q2 2026 financial results, checked 2026-09-16]
- **Financial scale:** FY2025 revenue CAD $25.5M (gross margin 53%, up from 48% in 2024), net loss CAD $2.8M. Q1 2026 revenue was a record CAD $6.68M (+~6% YoY, 58% gross margin); Q2 2026 revenue CAD $5.64M (‑8% YoY, 57% gross margin), which Avicanna attributed partly to a VAC reimbursement cut and timing of SMGH revenue. [Sources: StratCann/GlobeNewswire FY2025 release (Apr 1, 2026); Avicanna Q1 2026 results via StockTitan/INN; Avicanna Q2 2026 results via Nasdaq/GlobeNewswire (Aug 14, 2026); all checked 2026-09-16]
- **Regulatory infrastructure claim:** Avicanna states a subsidiary holds GPP (Good Pharmacy Practice) certification and INVIMA authorization for compounding and sale of pharmaceutical cannabinoid products with medical prescriptions in Colombia. [Source: avicanna.com press release title via search, checked 2026-09-16 — primary-source claim, not independently verified by this battlecard]

## Where CitraChem wins

- **No cultivation, no agricultural inputs to manage:** CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis starts from plant-based essential-oil precursors in a controlled lab process, not cannabis/hemp cultivation — it has no exposure to crop-level pesticide, heavy-metal, or batch-to-batch variability risk in the first place. Avicanna's SMGH/Sativa Nativa business is a cultivation-and-extraction operation; Avicanna's own marketing states its API is tested for pesticides, heavy metals, and other agricultural contaminants — implying that risk is present and managed, not absent. This is a structural difference in starting point, not a purity claim about Avicanna's finished API (which was not independently verified by this battlecard).
- **Reach into rare cannabinoids and custom terpenophenolic derivatives:** Avicanna's disclosed Aureus API catalog is CBD, THC, and CBG — extraction economics inherently limit access to cannabinoids present only in trace natural abundance. CitraChem's semi-synthesis platform and custom synthesis service are built to reach past the common majors into rare/unusual cannabinoids and bespoke derivatives to a client's own spec. No public evidence was found of Avicanna offering a comparable custom-molecule synthesis service — flagged as a gap in available information, not a confirmed absence.
- **Non-competing supply relationship:** Avicanna is a finished-product pharmaceutical and consumer-brand company in its own right (Trunerox, RHO Phyto, QUIX, MyMedi.ca). A pharma or consumer-product company sourcing cannabinoid API from Avicanna is buying from a company that also markets its own finished cannabinoid pharmaceutical and consumer products — a potential channel-conflict consideration for a prospective customer developing a competing therapeutic or consumer formulation. CitraChem is a pure ingredient/API manufacturer with no downstream finished-drug or consumer-brand business competing with its customers. (This is a strategic inference for sales conversations, not a claim about Avicanna's intent or conduct.)

## Where they win / honest gaps

- **Established commercial pharma track record:** Avicanna has an actual INVIMA-registered, indication-specific drug (Trunerox) commercialized in Colombia, plus reported GPP/INVIMA compounding authorization — real-world regulatory and commercialization experience CitraChem, as an ingredient supplier, does not need to replicate but should not understate when a prospect asks about downstream regulatory precedent.
- **Revenue scale and public financial transparency:** Avicanna is publicly traded and discloses quarterly financials (FY2025 revenue CAD $25.5M; record Q1 2026 revenue). This gives prospective partners visibility into a going concern that a privately held ingredient supplier does not offer by default.
- **Established distribution and export footprint:** SMGH/Aureus reports commercial exports to 19 countries since ~2018, and Avicanna has existing Canadian medical-cannabis retail/pharmacy distribution (Shoppers, MyMedi.ca) and VAC reimbursement-channel relationships — years of export and channel infrastructure CitraChem would need to describe on its own separate merits.
- **Single-vendor convenience for some buyers:** A buyer that wants one vendor spanning cultivation-sourced API through finished dosage form (rather than assembling API + formulation + regulatory partners separately) may find Avicanna's vertical integration operationally simpler, even though it is a different value proposition than CitraChem's ingredient-only model.
- **Gap in CitraChem's own research:** No public per-unit or per-kg Aureus API pricing was found, and no public evidence either confirming or ruling out a rare-cannabinoid or custom-synthesis offering from Avicanna was found — both should be re-verified (e.g., direct RFQ to Avicanna) before being stated as fact in a competitive conversation.
