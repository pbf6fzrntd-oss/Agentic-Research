# Battlecard: CB Therapeutics, Inc.

**Category:** Biosynthesis / precision fermentation route (alternative to CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis)
**Last checked:** 2026-09-16

## Summary
CB Therapeutics is a private synthetic-biology company (founded 2015, San Diego/Carlsbad, CA) that produces cannabinoids, tryptamines (including psychedelic compounds like psilocybin, psilocin, DMT), ergoline-class compounds, and (more recently) food-tech colors/antioxidants using **precision fermentation** — engineered yeast (and, per some secondary sources, other microbial/cell-free systems) fed on sugar, with no cannabis or coca-type cultivation and no plant extraction involved. This is a genuinely different route from CitraChem's: CitraChem starts from plant-based essential-oil precursors and does biomimetic semi-synthesis, whereas CB Therapeutics builds cannabinoid pathways into microbes and ferments them from simple feedstock (sugar) — the same general "de novo biosynthesis" category as Willow Biosciences-style platforms, not agricultural extraction and not classical total organic synthesis.

CB Therapeutics markets itself broadly across cannabinoids, psychedelics/tryptamines, and (as of 2024–2025) ergolines and food-tech ingredients — a wider and more diffuse molecule portfolio than CitraChem's cannabinoid/terpene/terpenophenolic focus.

## Pricing (source + date)
No public price list found for CB Therapeutics cannabinoid or tryptamine products as of 2026-09-16 (multiple web searches of their site content, press releases, and third-party coverage). Like CitraChem, they appear to operate on a direct/quote or partnership basis rather than a public catalog with list prices. **Note:** CitraChem's own model is likewise quote-based per `CLAUDE.md` — this is not a differentiator either direction on pricing model; the actual quoted numbers on either side are unverified and should never be assumed.

Treat the $395M figure as **unverified and likely inaccurate** — flagging it here so no other agent repeats it as fact.

## Features
(Company-claimed capabilities, sourced to press releases and one venture-database aggregation; CitraChem could not independently verify purity/COA data since cbthera.com and several trade-press domains were inaccessible during this research session — see Sources.)

- **Platform:** engineered-yeast precision fermentation producing cannabinoids, tryptamines/psychedelic compounds, ergolines, and food-tech colors/antioxidants from simple sugar feedstock — no cannabis cultivation, no plant extraction. Company states it was "the first biotech company in the world" to produce CBD and tryptamine molecules (psilocybin, DMT) in yeast (company claim, via [MyJoyOnline profile](https://www.myjoyonline.com/southern-california-based-cb-therapeutics-develops-eco-conscious-solutions-through-precision-fermentation/), undated but indexed with recent coverage; primary company statement not independently re-verified this session).
- **Molecule breadth:** cannabinoids (CBD, CBG, CBC, CBN, THCV reported by secondary sources), tryptamines/psychedelics (psilocybin, psilocin, DMT, MDMA analogs), ergoline-class compounds (added January 2025), and food-tech colors/antioxidants (added April 2024) — a materially broader (and less cannabinoid-specific) molecule scope than CitraChem's phytocannabinoid/terpene/terpenophenolic focus.
- **Clinical-supply collaboration:** entered a collaboration/MOU with **Cleveland Clinic**, announced 2020-04-20, to manufacture psilocybin, DMT, and MDMA analog compounds for clinical trials targeting depression, addiction, and PTSD ([BusinessWire, 2020-04-20](https://www.biospace.com/article/releases/cb-therapeutics-enters-into-collaboration-agreement-with-cleveland-clinic-for-the-manufacture-of-psilocybin-dmt-and-mdma-therapeutic-compounds-for-clinical-trials-to-treat-depression-addiction-and-ptsd/)). This is a psychedelics/tryptamine deal, not a cannabinoid deal — no named cannabinoid customer was found in this research.
- **Facility:** expanded into a 16,000 sq ft R&D/production facility in Carlsbad, CA in April 2022, with bioreactor suites for precision fermentation and automated process-development robotics; staff reported at 21, growing toward 26 by end of 2022 ([GlobeNewswire, 2022-04-25](https://www.globenewswire.com/en/news-release/2022/04/25/2428142/0/en/CB-Therapeutics-rapidly-expands-cellular-agriculture-capabilities-at-new-facility.html)).
- **Funding/backers:** Series A closed May 2021, filled entirely by re.Mind Capital (a fund within Christian Angermayer's Apeiron family office); board additions at that time included Dr. Srinivas Rao (CSO, Atai Life Sciences) and Dr. Jeff Miller (CEO, Invivoscribe) ([BusinessWire, 2021-05-10](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210510005112/en/CB-Therapeutics-Closes-on-Oversubscribed-Series-A)). Earlier alumnus of Y Combinator's Summer 2018 cohort; company itself dates founding to 2015 ([San Diego Business Journal](https://www.sdbj.com/technology/cb-therapeutics-move/), via web search summary).
- **Recency/operating status:** press activity as recently as January 2025 (ergoline platform expansion announcement) indicates the company was still active and publishing news at that point; this research could not confirm operating status past that beyond low-confidence secondary mentions (a marketing-consultancy site referencing a mid-2026 "Satellite Consultancy" partnership and a Roche pRED meeting) that could not be verified against a primary source and should be treated as unconfirmed.
- **No pharma-grade/GMP certification could be independently verified.** Company press materials describe output as "pharmaceutical-grade" (self-described claim, e.g. in the Cleveland Clinic release and a May 2024 "Unveils Revolutionary Breakthroughs" release) — this is a company claim, not an independently confirmed certification, and CitraChem should not repeat it as fact.

## Where CitraChem wins
- **Cannabinoid/terpenophenolic specificity vs. diffuse molecule portfolio.** CB Therapeutics has spread its fermentation platform across cannabinoids, psychedelics/tryptamines, ergolines, and food-tech colors — CitraChem's platform is purpose-built around phytocannabinoids, terpenes, and custom terpenophenolic derivatives, which is a narrower and more defensible claim of depth for a pharma/nutraceutical buyer specifically sourcing cannabinoid API (per CLAUDE.md's differentiator: "platform generality extends past the common majors ... to rare/unusual cannabinoids and bespoke terpenophenolic derivatives").
- **Biomimetic route vs. de novo microbial pathway engineering.** CitraChem's semi-synthesis starts from plant-based essential-oil precursors and follows the plant's own biosynthetic logic (per CLAUDE.md), which CitraChem positions as fewer steps and faster scale-up than building/tuning a full heterologous enzyme pathway in yeast from sugar — no independently sourced yield/cost data exists on either side to confirm this quantitatively, so this should be framed as CitraChem's stated process rationale, not a proven cost advantage, until a specific number is verified.
- **Custom synthesis service for a client's own candidate molecule** is an explicit CitraChem offering (citrachem.com/custom/); no equivalent named "submit your own candidate molecule" service was found on CB Therapeutics' public materials in this research (absence of evidence, not confirmed absence — their site could not be fetched directly this session).
- **Transparency for AI/agent-discoverable research and regulated buyers.** CitraChem's CLAUDE.md-driven positioning requires every claim to survive a COA/patent check; no equivalent COA, purity spec, or patent-status disclosure was found for CB Therapeutics' cannabinoid line in this research.

## Where they win / honest gaps
- **Longer track record and larger disclosed team/facility than a newer entrant.** CB Therapeutics has operated since 2015, closed a Series A in 2021, expanded to a dedicated 16,000 sq ft Carlsbad facility in 2022, and has continued to publish platform-expansion news through at least January 2025 — a multi-year operating and fundraising history that CitraChem should not understate.
- **Clinical-trial-grade supply relationship with a major medical institution (Cleveland Clinic)** is a named, primary-sourced credibility marker (2020) that CitraChem has no directly comparable public equivalent for in this research; note it is a psychedelics/tryptamine deal, not a cannabinoid deal, so its relevance to cannabinoid-buyer decisions specifically is indirect.
- **Fermentation from sugar** is a genuinely distinct feedstock/process story (vs. CitraChem's essential-oil-derived precursors) that some sustainability-focused buyers may prefer — CitraChem should not claim environmental superiority over fermentation without a sourced comparison, since none was found in this research.
- **Gap in this research, not necessarily in the company:** could not verify current pricing, purity/COA data, current catalog/SKU list, GMP or other quality certifications, or any named customer specifically for cannabinoid (as opposed to psychedelic) product, because cbthera.com and most trade-press domains were inaccessible to this research session (egress-blocked). ## Facts
- CB Therapeutics, Inc. is a private company producing cannabinoids, tryptamines/psychedelic compounds, ergolines, and food-tech colorants via engineered-yeast precision fermentation from sugar feedstock, not plant extraction or cultivation. Source: company-adjacent press coverage, multiple releases 2019–2025 (see Sources).
- Founded 2015; headquartered in Carlsbad, CA (previously San Diego, CA); co-founders reported as Sher Ali Butt and Jacob Vogan; Y Combinator Summer 2018 cohort alumnus. Source: San Diego Business Journal, via web search summary, checked 2026-09-16.
- CB Therapeutics entered a collaboration/MOU with Cleveland Clinic, announced 2020-04-20, to manufacture psilocybin, DMT, and MDMA analog compounds for FDA-related clinical trials in depression, addiction, and PTSD. Source: BusinessWire press release, 2020-04-20.
- CB Therapeutics closed an oversubscribed Series A round in May 2021, filled entirely by re.Mind Capital (a fund within Christian Angermayer's Apeiron family office); round amount was not publicly disclosed. Source: BusinessWire press release, 2021-05-10.
- CB Therapeutics expanded to a 16,000 sq ft R&D/production facility in Carlsbad, CA in April 2022, with dedicated fermentation bioreactor suites; staff reported at ~21–26. Source: GlobeNewswire press release, 2022-04-25.
- CB Therapeutics announced expansion of its platform into ergoline-class compound biosynthesis in January 2025. Source: AccessNewswire / Yahoo Finance coverage, dated January 2025.
- No public price list, purity/COA data, or GMP certification for CB Therapeutics' cannabinoid line was found or independently verified in this research (checked 2026-09-16); a claimed $395M January-2026 funding round found on one low-quality aggregator (trysignalbase.com) is unverified and contradicted by other funding-database summaries (~$7.6M total disclosed) — do not repeat the $395M figure as fact.
