GVB Biopharma
==============

**Category:** Agricultural (plant) extraction — hemp-derived cannabinoid isolate manufacturer, large-scale commodity/wholesale volume
**Last checked:** 2026-09-16

## Summary

GVB Biopharma is a large-scale hemp-derived cannabinoid ingredient manufacturer headquartered on a 20-acre production campus in Prineville, Oregon, where it runs extraction through finished-ingredient manufacturing in-house (cryogenic/ethanol extraction, short-path distillation, isolation). It is a volume-scale commodity player in the CBD/CBG/CBN isolate market — its own March 2026 release claims 12,000+ kg of combined CBD/CBG/CBN isolate produced and sold in that single month (30,000+ kg for Q1 2026) — not a semi-synthesis or rare-cannabinoid specialist. GVB is founder/employee-owned as of a late-2023 divestiture from Nasdaq-listed 22nd Century Group; this is the company's current ownership status per the most recent confirmed source below and should be re-verified on next refresh given the sector's ongoing consolidation.

## Capabilities

- In-house, vertically integrated: extraction → distillation → isolation → finished-ingredient manufacturing on one 20-acre Prineville, OR campus, with (per GVB's own claims found via search) farm-to-finished-ingredient traceability.
- Product line: CBD, CBG, CBN, CBDA, CBDV, CBT and other major/minor cannabinoid isolates and distillates, sold in bulk/wholesale quantities — a commodity-cannabinoid catalog, not a bespoke/rare-cannabinoid or custom-derivative service as far as found in this research pass.
- Certifications claimed (via search-engine summary of gvbbiopharma.com/science/, not independently fetched/verified this pass): ISO 9001:2015, food-grade and cGMP-compliant, NSF International GMP Registration for dietary supplements; described as an early Oregon Department of Agriculture food-grade-certified hemp processor.
- Extraction method: cryogenic/ethanol-based hemp biomass extraction followed by short-path distillation and isolation — an agricultural-extraction process, i.e. sourced from cannabis/hemp cultivation and crop biomass, not a semi-synthesis or fermentation-biosynthesis route.
- Scale: claims industry-leading throughput — 300,000+ kg/year isolate capacity per the Feb 2025 facility-launch release, and 12,000+ kg produced/sold in a single month (March 2026) per the April 2026 release.
- Ownership: acquired by Nasdaq-listed 22nd Century Group, Inc. in May 2022 (~$55M deal per search-result summaries of Cannabis Business Times/GlobeNewswire coverage); divested back into a founder/employee-affiliated entity ("Specialty Acquisition Corporation," a Nevada corporation affiliated with GVB employees) effective December 28, 2023, per search-result summaries of Cannabis Business Times coverage of the sale agreement. **This ownership chain is inferred from secondary search-result summaries of press coverage, not a direct read of a primary filing or the current gvbbiopharma.com "About" page** — the SEC exhibit and Lincoln International deal page that would confirm this directly could not be fetched due to network egress restrictions during this research pass. ## Where CitraChem wins

- **Purity/consistency mechanism, not just purity number:** GVB's 99%+ isolate purity comes from post-extraction refinement of hemp biomass, meaning it still inherits upstream agricultural variability (crop-to-crop, harvest-to-harvest) and carries inherent exposure to pesticide/heavy-metal/agricultural-impurity risk before purification — CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis starts from plant-based essential-oil precursors, not cannabis cultivation, avoiding that upstream variability and agricultural-impurity carryover by process design (see CLAUDE.md).
- **Molecule breadth beyond commodity majors:** GVB's public catalog centers on the common cannabinoids (CBD, CBG, CBN, CBDA, CBDV, CBT) at commodity/wholesale volume; nothing found in this research indicates a rare-cannabinoid catalog or a bespoke custom-synthesis service comparable to CitraChem's PhytoCules catalog + custom terpenophenolic derivative synthesis for a client's own candidate molecule.
- **No cannabis cultivation dependency:** GVB's supply chain is explicitly hemp-biomass-based (crop cultivation → extraction); CitraChem's process is designed to sidestep cannabis/hemp cultivation entirely, which matters to pharma/pharma-adjacent buyers concerned with agricultural supply-chain risk (weather, crop yield, regulatory status of cultivation) as a single point of failure.
- **Fit for regulated pharma/biotech buyers:** CitraChem's ICP and positioning (CLAUDE.md) is built around pre-clinical/clinical pharma and CRO buyers who need documented, batch-consistent API-grade material and a technical relationship-sales motion; GVB's public marketing (wholesale/bulk buyer's guides, "request a quote" bulk-buy pages) reads as commodity-ingredient B2B sales oriented toward nutraceutical/CPG-scale bulk buyers rather than a pharma-development technical relationship, based on the page titles and structure found in this research (not independently confirmed by reading the full pages).

## Where they win / honest gaps

- **Scale, honestly:** if the March 2026 and February 2025 release figures are accurate, GVB is producing and selling isolate at a volume (12,000+ kg/month; 300,000+ kg/year facility capacity) that is a commodity-manufacturing scale CitraChem's biomimetic semi-synthesis platform has not published a comparable public capacity figure against. For a buyer whose primary requirement is very large volumes of a common major cannabinoid (CBD, CBG, CBN) at commodity economics, GVB's scale claim is a real advantage worth naming honestly.
- **Vertical integration and track record:** GVB has operated hemp extraction at scale since well before this research window and claims a fully in-house extraction-through-finished-ingredient supply chain on one campus, plus GMP/ISO/NSF-type certifications (per its own claimed page, not independently verified this pass) — an established operational track record that a newer entrant has to earn.
