# Battlecard: Purisys / Noramco (now Siegfried)

**Last checked:** 2026-09-16

**Category:** Pharmaceutical-grade cannabinoid API manufacturer — hybrid agricultural-extraction-plus-chemical-synthesis route. CitraChem's most direct "vs. agricultural extraction / vs. semi-synthesis" competitor per `CLAUDE.md`.

**Why one file, not two:** Purisys was spun off from Noramco's cannabinoids business in October 2019, and as of May 1, 2026 both are again under single ownership (Siegfried Holding AG), alongside a third related entity, Extractas Bioscience. They appear to continue operating as distinct named business lines/sites under Siegfried (no evidence found of a merged single brand), so this card treats them as one ownership group with three specialized sites, noting where their roles differ.

---

## Summary

Purisys, LLC (Athens, Georgia) is the cannabinoid-API specialist spun out of Noramco in 2019: it manufactures pharmaceutical-grade CBD and >50 other major/minor cannabinoids, degradants, metabolites, and reference standards, and holds multiple commercial Drug Master Files (DMFs). Noramco, Inc. (Wilmington, Delaware) is the larger parent-heritage small-molecule/controlled-substance API manufacturer Purisys was carved out of. Extractas Bioscience (Westbury, Tasmania, Australia — formerly Tasmanian Alkaloids) is a plant-extraction specialist (opium poppy alkaloids and cannabis full-plant extracts/isolates) that was under the same former owner, SK Capital Partners, as part of the "Noramco Group." As of May 1, 2026, Siegfried Holding AG (a Swiss CDMO) completed its acquisition of the Noramco Group's drug-substance business (Noramco + Purisys) and Extractas Bioscience from SK Capital, bringing all three under one corporate owner. A fourth former Noramco Group company, Halo Pharma (drug-product CDMO, Whippany NJ / Mirabel QC), was spun out as an independent standalone company in the same restructuring and was **not** part of the Siegfried deal.

Notably, Purisys's own cannabinoid process is not simple agricultural extraction: Purisys describes its flagship CBD as **synthetically manufactured and "nature-identical"** via a patented chemical process (in production since 2016, <0.001% THC), and holds patents for converting hemp-derived CBD/THC into other cannabinoids (e.g., CBN) via halogenation chemistry. Extractas is the group's true agricultural-extraction node (cannabis and poppy full-plant extraction). So the combined group actually straddles both of CLAUDE.md's "agricultural extraction" and "other semi-synthesis" competitor categories — worth citing that way rather than as a pure extraction house.

## Capabilities (confirmed via primary/trade sources)

- Purisys produces cGMP cannabinoid APIs described as "synthetic" and "nature-identical" — over 50 major and minor cannabinoids, plus degradants, metabolites, and analytical reference standards. Source: Purisys company materials as summarized by MTEC and PharmaCompass listings (accessed via search index 2026-09-16; direct fetch blocked).
- Purisys holds multiple commercial Drug Master Files (DMFs) for cannabinoid APIs and describes itself as ICH Q7/EU GMP compliant with 4 commercially validated APIs. Source: pharmacompass.com Purisys LLC listing (accessed via search index 2026-09-16).
- Purisys's core CBD is manufactured via a patented chemical process (not plant extraction) yielding <0.001% THC, in production since 2016; the DEA removed this CBD from Schedule I of the Controlled Substances Act in November 2019. Source: PR Newswire, "Purisys Cannabinoid Ingredients Descheduled by U.S. Drug Enforcement Agency," Nov 2019 — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/purisys-cannabinoid-ingredients-descheduled-by-us-drug-enforcement-agency-300963340.html (accessed via search index 2026-09-16).
- Purisys holds US patents for converting hemp-derived CBD/THC into other cannabinoids (e.g., CBD → CBN, THC → CBN) via halogenation/cyclization chemistry — i.e., a semi-synthetic conversion route from a cannabis-plant-derived starting material, not a from-scratch or non-cannabis-precursor route. Sources: US Patent 11,667,619 ("Synthesis and purification of cannabinol from cannabidiol"), Justia Patents — https://patents.justia.com/patent/11667619; US20220267290A1, Google Patents — https://patents.google.com/patent/US20220267290A1/en (accessed 2026-09-16).

## Where CitraChem wins

- **Non-cannabis starting material vs. cannabis-derived precursor conversion.** Purisys's rarer-cannabinoid production (e.g., CBN) chemically converts hemp/cannabis-derived CBD or THC via halogenation reactions — meaning its supply chain still runs through a cannabis-plant-derived, DEA-controlled-substance-regulated intermediate for much of its portfolio. CitraChem's biomimetic route starts from non-cannabis plant essential-oil precursors, avoiding cannabis cultivation and (per `CLAUDE.md`) the associated agricultural batch variability at the front end of the process.
- **Platform breadth for bespoke/novel molecules.** CLAUDE.md positions CitraChem's custom-synthesis service (novel/rare cannabinoids and bespoke terpenophenolic derivatives to a client's own spec, submitted via citrachem.com/custom/) as differentiated from suppliers whose catalog — however broad (Purisys claims >50 cannabinoids) — is still a fixed catalog rather than an open-ended custom-molecule service. No evidence found that Purisys offers an equivalent client-directed custom-derivative design service; this should be verified against Purisys's current site on next refresh rather than asserted further.
- **Ownership stability during a live acquisition integration.** Purisys/Noramco customers are working with a supplier mid-integration into a new parent (Siegfried, closed May 2026) — a period where roadmap, account-team, and pricing continuity questions are live and reasonable for a prospect to ask about. This is a timing/context point, not a capability claim.

## Where they win / honest gaps

- **Decades-long pharmaceutical API track record and DMF portfolio.** Noramco has ~40+ years as a controlled-substance/small-molecule API manufacturer; Purisys holds multiple commercial DMFs specifically for cannabinoid APIs — DMF holdership is a concrete regulatory asset a preclinical/clinical customer's regulatory team will value, and CitraChem should not claim parity here without citing its own DMF status (not established in CLAUDE.md as of this writing — flag for the team, don't imply CitraChem has one).
- **Commercial-scale capacity.** Noramco's Wilmington, DE site is described as "large, commercial-scale" — combined with Siegfried's backing (a global CDMO), the group now has meaningfully larger balance-sheet and manufacturing-scale resources than a standalone smaller supplier. CitraChem's own scale claims should be verified before making a head-to-head scale comparison.
- **Public regulatory precedent.** Purisys's CBD was the subject of a public DEA descheduling action (Nov 2019) and it has disclosed IND-track customer relationships (CNBX) — visible proof points of working inside FDA/DEA-regulated drug-development pipelines that a risk-averse buyer may weight heavily.
- **Now backed by a larger CDMO parent (Siegfried).** Post-acquisition, customers get access to Siegfried's broader small-molecule CDMO network, which may appeal to buyers wanting a single vendor across API and drug-product manufacturing (though note Halo Pharma, the drug-product CDMO, was spun out separately and is *not* part of the Siegfried group — a nuance worth confirming with any prospect who assumes full-service continuity).
