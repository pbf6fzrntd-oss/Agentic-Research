## 2. Verified findings

- **NCT06148038 — "CBD for Breast Cancer Primary Tumors"** (Phase 1a "Window Trial"): sponsored by MUSC, PI Dr. Nancy DeMore (contact per search results: demore@musc.edu). Status: **Recruiting**, estimated start 2026-09-15, up to 84 patients with invasive breast cancer, randomized 2:2:1:1 (CBD low dose / CBD high dose / matched low-dose placebo / matched high-dose placebo). Primary objective: whether oral CBD produces measurable biological change (Ki67 proliferation marker) in primary tumor tissue before surgical resection. Primary completion estimated 2028-03-25. (Source: clinicaltrials.gov listing and Hollings Cancer Center's own trial page — WebSearch snippets; not independently page-verified via direct fetch due to `EGRESS_BLOCKED` pattern on this project.)
- **Related, MUSC-adjacent trial base:** a separate pilot study of CBD for aromatase-inhibitor-associated musculoskeletal symptoms in breast cancer patients (published, per PMC/University of Iowa co-authorship) reports CBD use as safe and prompts the further study above — suggesting an established, multi-study CBD-in-breast-cancer research thread at this institution, not a one-off trial.
- **Broader literature base is real, not just speculative:** a 2025 review ("Cannabidiol as a novel therapeutic agent in breast cancer: evidence from literature," PubMed ID 40275168) and multiple other active trials (a Phase III CBD-for-arthralgia trial, an anxiety-in-advanced-breast-cancer RCT) indicate this is an active, multi-site area of clinical interest, not confined to MUSC alone. CBD's preclinical anti-proliferative signal is best characterized in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC).
- **Drug source not confirmed for NCT06148038 specifically.** A related MUSC-associated trial in the same therapeutic thread used Epidiolex (100 mg BID), the FDA-approved CBD product — which is a Jazz Pharmaceuticals/GW Pharma product. It is plausible but **not confirmed** that the Window Trial uses Epidiolex as well, given it is the only FDA-approved oral CBD product and the easiest regulatory path for an academic IND. This materially affects the fit read below — flagged as unconfirmed rather than assumed.
