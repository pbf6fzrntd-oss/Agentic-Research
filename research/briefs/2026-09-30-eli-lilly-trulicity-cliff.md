## 1. Target & angle

Eli Lilly's Trulicity (dulaglutide), named in `research/OPPORTUNITY-MAP.md`'s patent-cliff table as a 2027 event with ~$7.1B in annual revenue at risk, sits inside Lilly's much larger and still-growing diabetes/obesity franchise. The angle worth testing: Trulicity's genericization lands squarely inside two indications on CitraChem's own disease-target matrix — **Diabetes** and **Obesity** — and CitraChem already runs an active internal research program in exactly that indication pair (THCV, with Hebrew University, "Obesity / Type 2 Diabetes," per the Opportunity Map's research-programs table). That's a concrete internal asset to anchor a conversation on, not a cold indication-matching exercise.

## 2. Verified findings

**Trulicity patent timing and revenue trajectory (confirmed):** Trulicity's US exclusivity runs through 2027 (compound patent and biologics data protection, with device-related protections extending further). Sales have already been declining fast on an internal basis — not primarily from generic competition, but from cannibalization by Lilly's own newer GLP-1/GIP franchise: Trulicity fell from ~$7.4B (2022) to ~$5.2B (2024), while Mounjaro (tirzepatide) reached ~$11.5B in 2024. [SingleCare, labiotech.eu, Oregon PDAB summary via WebSearch]

**Lilly's actual follow-on strategy (confirmed, and it's not cannabinoid-adjacent):** Lilly's answer to the Trulicity gap is its own next-generation GLP-1/GIP pipeline — orforglipron (oral, ~12.4% weight loss at 72 weeks in Attain-1) and retatrutide (a GGG tri-agonist, up to 22.6% body-weight reduction in late-stage trials, with a regulatory filing planned for Q1 2027) — plus Zepbound/Mounjaro already on market. [Fool.com, Pluang, GuruFocus via WebSearch] This confirms the $7.1B "at risk" framing is real but also that Lilly is not leaving this revenue gap unaddressed — it's filling it with its own peptide-agonist portfolio, not looking externally for a replacement mechanism.

**No current Lilly cannabinoid/ECS pipeline activity found.** Searches for Lilly + cannabinoid/endocannabinoid turned up no active program, acquisition, or public statement — the only recent large Lilly R&D collaboration surfaced (InnoCare Pharma, up to $3.35B, announced Sept 24 2026) is for undisclosed non-cannabinoid targets. **This is a "not found," not a confirmed absence** — Lilly's full pipeline was not exhaustively page-verified (EGRESS_BLOCKED risk on lilly.com not tested directly; relying on WebSearch snippets per the standing caveat on this project).

**No current named BD contact identified.** No specific R&D/BD executive or open-innovation intake channel was verified in this pass for the diabetes/obesity therapeutic area specifically (Lilly does run a general external-innovation program, lilly.com/innovation, but that wasn't independently page-verified here).

## 3. Fit assessment

**Moderate-to-good fit, but indication-level, not molecule-level.** Two matrix indications hit at once (Diabetes, Obesity) is unusual — most candidates in this sweep hit zero or one. The genuinely differentiating point is that CitraChem isn't starting from scratch here: the THCV/Hebrew University obesity-diabetes program (per the Opportunity Map) gives CitraChem real, citable derivative-development work in this exact space to open a conversation with, rather than a generic "we sell pure cannabinoids" pitch.

That said, be honest about the size of the reach: Lilly's own chosen mechanism for this disease space is GLP-1/GIP peptide agonism, not small-molecule ECS modulation — there's no public signal Lilly is even considering a cannabinoid/terpenoid route for metabolic disease. The realistic opening is not "replace Trulicity's revenue," it's a **custom-synthesis/IP-partnership conversation** positioned around CitraChem's existing THCV obesity/T2D derivative work as a complementary or comparator asset — most plausibly pitched to a Lilly external-innovation/scouting function rather than the core GLP-1 franchise team. This is speculative and would need a named, current contact before it's actionable.
