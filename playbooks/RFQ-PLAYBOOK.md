# RFQ Playbook

Default motion for all CitraChem GTM agents when the target is the technical/relationship buyer (see `CLAUDE.md` ICP). Deviate only when the task explicitly says otherwise (e.g. a large pharma partnership negotiation that's clearly beyond a single agent's scope).

## Principles
1. **Sample or custom-synthesis inquiry is the primary CTA, always.** "Request a sample" or "submit your candidate molecule" beats "buy now" for a regulated ingredient a scientist has to validate before anyone commits budget. Only offer a call when the prospect asks for one or the ask clearly needs scoping (a multi-molecule custom synthesis project, a supply-agreement volume).
2. **Longer cycles than a self-serve SaaS motion, but still proactive.** A pharma/CPG R&D buyer moves in weeks, often through a scientist plus a separate procurement step — don't chase daily, but don't go quiet either. Sequences are 3 touches across roughly three weeks, not 6-8 enterprise-procurement touches.
3. **Speak to the scientist first, procurement second.** Lead technical messages (purity, process, COA, patent status) at the R&D/chemistry persona; only bring pricing/procurement language in once they've engaged.
4. **Lead with what fits the ask**, not the biggest possible deal: a sample request for evaluation, a specific PhytoCule off the catalog, or a custom synthesis scoping conversation for a novel molecule — don't push a supply agreement on a first touch.
5. **Evidence-forward, not hype-forward.** State purity/process facts plainly (cite `CLAUDE.md`'s differentiators and any relevant COA/battlecard); don't over-claim ahead of what a scientist can verify.

## Default outbound sequence (outbound-sdr)
- **Touch 1**: Signal-based, short (under 150 words). One concrete, true observation (a publication, a patent filing, a product launch, a hiring signal for a chemist/formulator) tied to why CitraChem's platform is relevant, plus a "request a sample" or "tell us your target molecule" CTA. No pricing needed yet.
- **Touch 2** (if no reply, ~7 days later): A new, specific angle — a differentiator relevant to their stated work (purity data, a rare-cannabinoid capability, custom synthesis for their kind of molecule), still short, same CTA.
- **Touch 3** (final, ~10 days after touch 2): Direct "should I close the loop on this" — low-pressure, keeps the door open, no more chasing after this.

## Default inbound flow (inbound-rfq)
- Default reply answers the technical question first (purity, availability, process, lead time framing) before anything else.
- Point to a sample request or the custom synthesis form (citrachem.com/custom/) as the next concrete step; only mention pricing as "quote-based, scoped per project" per `CLAUDE.md` — never invent a number.
- Only propose a call if they ask for one, or their message describes a scenario genuinely worth 15-30 minutes (a custom synthesis project with several unknowns, a potential supply agreement, a partnership-shaped inquiry like Waystone's).

## Default onboarding/expansion (customer-success)
- Onboarding goal after a first sample or order ships: confirm they received it, offer the COA/spec documentation proactively, and surface the natural next step (moving from sample to commercial quantity, or scoping a custom derivative) — one concrete next action, not a feature tour.
- Expansion nudge from sample → commercial quantity: trigger off the customer's own stated progress (their assay/formulation work succeeded, they're scaling a study), not a calendar date.
- Reorder/renewal nudge: trigger off their known consumption pattern or an approaching study/production milestone they've mentioned, not a blind check-in.

## Objection handling notes
- "We already source from an extraction supplier": don't disparage extraction broadly — point at the specific, verifiable difference (batch-to-batch purity/impurity profile, see `CLAUDE.md`'s vs.-extraction differentiator) and offer a side-by-side sample/COA comparison rather than asserting superiority.
- "We need a fully synthetic / GMP-validated source": be honest about CitraChem's current stage (2024 was its inaugural operational year) and route validation/regulatory-specific questions to a human rather than overpromising GMP or regulatory status not confirmed in `CLAUDE.md`.
- "We'll just have our own chemists synthesize it": legitimate for a team with in-house synthetic capacity and time; be honest about that tradeoff (time, yield, cost of de novo synthesis vs. CitraChem's biomimetic route) rather than oversell against it.
